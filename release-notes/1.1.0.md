The footer meters update live on Paseo 0.16 and later instead of checking every five seconds.

### Improved
- On Paseo 0.16 and later the daemon pushes each new reading to the footer meters, pill and popover, so they update the moment a sample is taken and stop costing anything while nobody is watching. If the live feed is refused or drops, the meters fall back to checking every five seconds and reconnect on their own. Paseo 0.15 keeps the five-second checks.
